Richup.io Rules & Guide

🎯 Goal: Bankrupt your opponents by buying properties, collecting rent, and constructing houses and hotels!

🎲 Rolling Doubles: If you roll doubles, you get another turn! Rolling 3 consecutive doubles sends you straight to Jail.

🏡 Monopolies & Houses: Collect all properties in a color group to achieve a Monopoly. You can then build up to 4 houses and 1 hotel per property evenly across the group. Rent multiplies dramatically!

⚖️ Auctions: If an unowned property is declined, it is immediately auctioned off to the highest bidder starting from $10.

🤝 Trading: You can propose trades anytime with other players for properties and cash.

🅿️ Free Parking: Taxes and fines collected are deposited into the Jackpot pool. The player who lands on Free Parking wins the entire pot!
